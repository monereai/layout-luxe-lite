# Treasure adventure rules
- Human map NPCs use bundled full-body artwork rather than bust emoji; preserve each sprite's aspect ratio. Why: quest characters must show their legs and feet without distortion.
- Quest completion derives from saved rescued state and pauses gameplay; explicit restart serializes pending actions, resets adventure/cache/canvas state via authenticated treasure_restart, and never clears reward ledgers or account inventory. Why: completion and replay must not loop or duplicate rewards.
- Developer reset uses a separate verified-identity/protected-role self-only SQL action, serializes pending movement, invalidates unfinished mini-game rounds and replaces saved/local/canvas progress without clearing payouts or account possessions. Why: anytime testing must not grant public reset privileges or replay rewards.
- Treasure tap routes use the shared collision pathfinder; locked answer stones route to the inner-door approach and explain prerequisites. Why: visible scenery must not silently swallow navigation or bypass gate rules.
- Tree interactions pause world/side-land controls while the browser-only Phaser harvesting dialog is open; safe expulsion restores only position, never quest progress or inventory. Why: maze keyboard input must not also move the world player.
- All tree interactions use the shared PacmanHarvestMiniGame onComplete callback carrying collected Energy and its replay trace; the HUD accepts only settled account Energy, never a client-reported reward. Why: legacy direct harvest paths must not bypass the maze or verified payouts.
- Crystal Gate and World Map Crystal entry call the same direct maze transition, never mount SideZone for caverns, and return to the main map on settlement or entry failure. Why: a failed or delayed round must not expose the retired cave/tree interaction.
- Crystal practice uses SQL-owned rounds when the tree is harvested or daily entries are exhausted, settles no Energy, and may grant the ravine scroll only after server replay reaches its maze cell; legacy untracked practice grants nothing. Why: daily harvest caps must not strand the scroll or trust a client claim.
- The third scroll appears only in the Shadow Ravine crystal maze; its cell is shared by rendering and server replay, and service-only settlement atomically grants it once from the round’s saved origin. Why: entry, other mazes and repeated settlement cannot bypass the prize.
- Smooth world movement eases vector velocity along collision-approved corridor endpoints with buffered edge turns and release friction; authenticated sprint/dash spend Energy before enabling boosts, with bounded retries and cancellation on pause/reset. Maze animation preserves its deterministic tick trace. Why: animation must not forge travel, Energy or rewards or repeatedly charge after input stops.
- Save-specific huntSpots select three key-fragment digs from the shared twelve-coordinate pool; SQL generates and validates account placements while existing progressed saves retain their old positions. Why: replayability must not strand gear quests or invalidate saved collections.
- Harvest best scores are account-keyed local display records, never a source for Energy or account rewards. Why: local persistence must not become a payment or reward authority.
- Supporter buttons use the shared SUPPORTER_PAYMENT_URL configuration and open a separate tab without granting benefits. Why: donations remain separate from membership and wallet purchases.
- Active WorldCanvas drives independent timed walking poses generated from equipped turnaround art, and reads the shared Energy balance before sprinting. Why: directional frames must not masquerade as a walk cycle or allow boosts after Energy runs out.

- Continue Daily Hunts refreshes only key-fragment targets once per UTC day at the chapter door and preserves equipment, gates and reward-ledger references. Why: renewed exploration must not replay account rewards.
- Visible quest seeds render above the fog layer while collected-item filtering still removes them; pickup locations and prerequisites stay mirrored in authenticated SQL. Why: visible objectives must remain discoverable without bypassing collection validation.
- Register image-based pickups in WorldCanvas's scenery cleanup and stop their tweens before destruction. Why: collected seeds and bells must disappear rather than accumulating stale sprites on each state refresh.
- Garden travel uses the main saved adventure and v6 authenticated movement/portal actions; the bridge extension uses zone-specific bounds and Vernon’s completed delivery unlock. Why: a new optional land must preserve progress and cannot bypass quest validation.
- Feather the Meadow extension over the existing southern bank without rescaling its original terrain; garden artwork retains collision-aligned landmarks. Why: continuous scenery must not move the bridge or change saved paths.
- Garden entry mounts the isolated barrel challenge and pauses world controls; jumps reserve shared Energy immediately and settle each accepted jump once in ordered service-only commits; failed spends pause with retry. Why: network delay must not enable overspending or unverified jumps.
- Garden startup exposes a ready Start action, also accepts arrow/WASD or touch input, and owns keyboard events without Phaser keyboard capture. Why: a waiting scene must not appear frozen or conflict with the paused world engine.
- Garden keyboard and touch jumps share one queued action and use the same fixed-step airborne duration as authenticated replay. Why: visible jumps and accepted rewards must not diverge.
- The barrel challenge prize is the first (meadow) scroll, shown above the top hopper and granted idempotently by garden_barrel_commit and the guest fallback; the one-time +50 Energy/+20 Coins also still apply. Why: the reward must match what the player sees and never duplicate on replay.
- The live client calls treasure_v6_action/treasure_v6_travel; every game-rule change (items, NPCs, dig rewards) must update v6, never only the stale v5 copy kept for older published clients. Why: editing v5 alone leaves signed-in players on old rules, as with Connie's apples and the dig Energy reward.
- Connie's fruit catcher opens after apple delivery regardless of scroll ownership; atomic service-only settlement grants its scroll, strict win flag and separate adventure Coins. Why: retries must not duplicate prizes or confuse Coins with Shop Points.
- Adventure Coins come only from the protected coin ledger; guest equivalents stay local. Fruits derive from unique quest apples held; mini-game catches are round-only. Energy overflow settles inside each verified gain transaction, never from a client-submitted amount; account storage is a mirror. Why: all counters must agree without forged points or replay bonuses.
- Only verified mini-game completion grants the first two scrolls; Connie completion persists its own strict boolean, and save retry retains the winning trace and round. Why: old scroll possession and network failure must not bypass or lose a win.
